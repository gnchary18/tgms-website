Admissions

How to join TGMS Dammannapet

Important: Dates, eligibility and the application link change every year. Always check the latest notice on the Notices page and the official Telangana education portal before applying.
Process

Steps to apply

  1. Read the notification. Check the class, age limit, seats and last date.
  2. Fill the application. Apply through the official online link or the school office, as announced.
  3. Entrance / selection. If an entrance test or merit list is announced, appear on the given date.
  4. Certificate verification. Bring the original documents and one set of copies.
  5. Confirm admission. Complete the formalities within the stated time.
Checklist

Documents usually needed

  • Birth certificate / date of birth proof
  • Previous class study / transfer certificate
  • Caste and income certificates, if applicable
  • Aadhaar card of the student (and parent, if asked)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Any other document listed in the current notification
