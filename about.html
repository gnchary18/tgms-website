Our school

TGMS Dammannapet

TGMS Dammannapet is a Telangana Model School. Model Schools were started to give students in rural and semi-urban areas the kind of English-medium education that is usually found only in big towns.

Our teachers work closely with each child, and our classrooms, laboratory and library help students learn by doing, not only by memorising.

Vision & mission

What guides us

👁️

Vision

To be a school where every child, whatever their background, grows into a confident, capable and responsible citizen.

🎯

Mission

To provide good teaching, a safe campus and equal opportunities, and to prepare students for higher education and life.

💎

Values

Honesty, discipline, respect, hard work, teamwork and care for the environment and community.
