About us

Education that opens doors

TGMS Dammannapet is part of the Telangana Model Schools network, created to give students from rural areas access to good English-medium education close to home.

  • Focus on strong basics in language, mathematics and science
  • Preparation for board exams and future competitive exams
  • Life skills, values and leadership through school activities
